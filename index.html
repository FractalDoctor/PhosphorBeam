△ PHOSPHORBEAM

Amateur Radio Satellite Pass Predictor.

Plan your amateur radio satellite contacts through the warm glow of a vintage phosphor terminal. PhosphorBeam predicts satellite passes from your location, calculates real-time Doppler frequency correction for uplink and downlink, and tracks active amateur birds on a 3D globe — all in authentic retro CRT style.

FEATURES

  • 3D globe with real-time satellite positions, radio footprints, and ground station marker
  • Pass predictions for all favourited satellites with quality ratings
  • Real-time Doppler correction for uplink and downlink frequencies
  • Sky track polar plots and corrected frequency charts per pass
  • Transponder database: FM repeaters, SSB/CW linear transponders, digital modes
  • 23 bundled amateur satellites with offline support
  • Automatic satellite discovery from AMSAT active frequency database
  • SGP4 orbital propagation with CelesTrak TLE updates
  • Maidenhead grid locator display
  • Green phosphor and amber CRT colour themes with scanline effects
  • No accounts, no tracking, no clutter

DATA SOURCES

Orbital elements are provided by CelesTrak (public domain). Transponder frequencies are sourced from the AMSAT / palewire amateur satellite database.
